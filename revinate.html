Enterprise SaaS: Revinate

HQ: Palo Alto, CA

-  The global hotel industry is massive, projected to hit over $1T by 2027. Revinate is tapping into the growing need for better guest engagement and personalized experiences in a *very* old-fashioned market, which is a multi-billion-dollar market in itself. As more hotels go digital, their tools will be in even higher demand

- In a MarTech world where players are trying to be an all-in-one solution, specialisation is key. Revinate's bull's eye focus on hospitality lets them win big. Consolidation is no doubt coming soon in the industry. Being a leader in the huge hospitality CRM space helps Revinate fend off competitors but also drives up valuations, depending on their appetite for further investment or even an acquisition

-  Hotels want to cut down on paying high commissions to OTAs like Booking.com. Revinate gives them the tools to bring in more direct bookings by helping hotels connect with guests directly, eliminating the middle-man, translating to higher profits as well as richer customer data within their control for the hotels

- Personalisation at scale. This is huge. They use data to help hotels understand their guests better and deliver personalized experiences, which is what people expect nowadays. This sets them apart and gives hotels a way to boost guest satisfaction and loyalty, something that's becoming essential to stay competitive

- Strong LTV. By focusing on creating better guest experiences, Revinate helps hotels keep their guests coming back. This increases the overall value each guest brings to a hotel, which makes the platform a valuable investment for hotels aiming to build long-term relationships

-  Revinate raised $45M in Series E funding in 2018, with over $70 million total from investors like Sozo Ventures, Benchmark Capital, and Tenaya Capital
