[Media: Qloo]

HQ: New York City, NY

- Sitting in the intersection between tech and media, Qloo leverages AI to analyse cultural preferences across categories like music, movies, fashion, and dining. With over 575M cultural correlations in its database, Qloo provides highly specific, actionable insights that help brands and companies personalise their offerings more effectively than traditional market research

- Qloo's platform is used across a variety of sectors, from $2T retail to $800B entertainment and $500B hospitality industries, making its insights applicable to media companies, marketers, urban planners, and more. This cross-industry application gives Qloo broad market reach and growth potential

- Personalisation is a multi-billion dollar opportunity, with brands increasingly investing in it to stand out in a crowded market. The global personalisation software market is projected to reach $2.2B by 2026, and Qloo's ability to personalise based on cultural preferences gives it a competitive edge in helping companies tailor their content and products

- Qloo has raised over $28M from investors such as Bluestone Equity, and high-profile names like Leonardo DiCaprio and Barry Sternlicht, founder of Starwood Hotels, suggesting investor confidence

- In a world where companies are shifting from demographic-based marketing to personalized and behavior-driven strategies, Qloo's ability to provide deep, context-rich insights into consumer preferences gives it a competitive advantage in helping brands build long-term loyalty and customer engagement
